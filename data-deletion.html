Data Deletion Request

United Gamers · Purrfect Cafe Merge

United Gamers provides a way for users to request deletion of personal data associated with Purrfect Cafe Merge.

How to request deletion

Email: infounitedgamers@gmail.com

Send an email with the subject Data Deletion Request – Purrfect Cafe Merge. Include the name or account information you use in the game so we can identify the relevant account.

What happens next

We will review the request, verify that it relates to the requester, and delete or anonymize applicable personal data associated with the account, subject to information we are legally required to retain.

Need help?

For privacy or data-deletion questions, contact infounitedgamers@gmail.com.
